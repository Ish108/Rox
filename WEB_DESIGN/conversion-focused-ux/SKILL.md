# Conversion-Focused UX & CRO

> **Objective:** Ensure every website is a commercial engine — turning high-craft visual attention into measurable inquiries, bookings, trials, calls, or sales without ever sacrificing conversion clarity for visual experimentation.

## 1. Business-First Discovery (Mandatory Before Designing)

Never design a single section until these 7 commercial truths are clear:
1. **Core Offer & Entry Point:** What is sold, and what is the lowest-friction entry step (Free Trial, Intro Session, Free Quote, Demo, Table Reservation, Purchase)?
2. **Ideal Customer & Traffic Source:** Who buys, what device are they on (e.g., 70%+ mobile Instagram/Google Ads for local/fitness vs. desktop B2B)?
3. **Core Pain & Transformation:** What concrete outcome does the buyer actually want?
4. **True Differentiation:** Real credentials, lineage, methodology, guarantees, speed, or results.
5. **Primary Conversion Objective:** The #1 measurable action every page guides toward.
6. **Top 3–5 Objections:** Fear, confusion, pricing anxiety, beginner intimidation, trust doubts.
7. **Industry Trust Signals:** What actually proves legitimacy in this niche?

> **Integrity Rule:** Never invent fake testimonials, fabricated review counts, fake awards, or bogus client logos. Use verified business facts or clearly labeled placeholder slots for client approval.

## 2. High-Converting Page Architecture

Every commercial page follows a deliberate persuasion arc:
1. **Above-the-Fold Hero:** Transformation/offer-led headline + concise 1–2 line clarity + unmistakable Primary CTA (action + low-risk microcopy) + optional Secondary CTA + authentic visual proof.
2. **Immediate Trust Strip:** Verified ratings, real credentials, client logos, or tangible numbers right below the fold.
3. **Benefit-Driven Services / Programs:** Explain outcomes rather than generic feature lists, paired with real imagery, clear differentiation, and contextual CTAs.
4. **Proof & Social Validation:** Specific before/after stories, coach/founder credentials, case studies, or customer reviews directly addressing buyer doubts.
5. **Objection Crushing (Pricing / Schedule / FAQ):** Remove uncertainty around cost, timing, beginner readiness, or process.
6. **Frictionless Conversion Mechanism:** Pre-filled WhatsApp Click-to-Chat (`https://wa.me/<number>?text=...`), minimalist 2–4 field lead form, lazy-loaded booking widget (Cal.com/Calendly/Mindbody/OpenTable), or streamlined checkout — plus sticky thumb-zone CTA bar on mobile for high-intent local/service/gym traffic.

## 3. Industry-Specific Adaptation Mandate

A combat sports academy and an enterprise SaaS platform have completely different buyer psychologies. Always load the exact playbook for the client's vertical from [conversion-framework.md](../../references/conversion-framework.md):
- **Gyms, Boxing & MMA Academies:** Mobile-first, authentic class video, coach lineage, beginner-friendly objection crushing, weekly timetable clarity, pre-filled **WhatsApp Free Trial CTA** + 2-field trial form + sticky mobile bar.
- **Local Services / Restaurants / E-Commerce / SaaS / B2B / Agencies / Startups / Professional Services:** Dedicated playbooks documented in [conversion-framework.md](../../references/conversion-framework.md).

---

## 4. Mandatory Conversion Evaluation Gate (Phase 7)

Score every finished website across all **9 CRO dimensions** in [conversion-framework.md](../../references/conversion-framework.md) (Value Proposition Clarity, CTA Visibility & Copy, Trust, User Journey, Friction Reduction, Mobile Conversion UX, Form Usability, Lead Capture Reliability, Content Hierarchy).

- **Pass Requirement:** Composite score `>= 85 / 100` AND no single dimension `< 8 / 10`.
